## 3. Conventions applied

- `temperature_c` is paired with a new column `temperature_type` (v4): `calcination` = final calcination, annealing or heat-treatment temperature (113 rows); `hydrothermal` = hydrothermal reaction temperature where the paper reports no calcination (14 rows: papers 16 and 17 and the hydrothermal rows of paper 8, the latter confirmed against the source in v5); `combustion` = furnace or reaction temperature of a combustion route without a separate calcination (10 rows: papers 12, 13, 15 and 23). The 14 combustion rows of paper 11 are `calcination` because the 500 °C step follows the combustion.
- `pH` is filled only when the paper reports a single synthesis-stage pH. Ranges (pH 2 to 3; 1.4 to 2) and peptization or washing pH values are described in `notes` and the column is left blank.
- `crystallite_size_nm` holds the size of the dominant phase as reported by the authors; phase fractions are in `phase`. Where the dominant phase cannot be established from the text, the size is blank and all reported sizes are in `notes`.
- Where a paper reports several band gaps for one sample, the value matching the method named in `measurement_method_band_gap` is entered and the others are in `notes`. Band gap methods are recorded as the authors state them; where the printed exponent and the text disagree, the row says so.
- Rows from commercial reference materials (P25, VWR anatase, commercial anatase) and from uncalcined samples without any temperature were not entered.
- No value was converted, interpolated or estimated.

Against the first-fit target (at least 200 rows, at least 20 per method): the row total is 63 short; sol-gel, combustion, hydrothermal and co-precipitation reach 20 rows, and solid-state is 13 short. Dominance of single papers is high in three classes: combustion 14 of 25 rows from one paper (Open Ceramics 2024, band gaps withheld, so only 7 combustion rows carry a band gap), solid-state 6 of 7 rows from one paper, and the 11-row Open Ceramics 2025 time series (BET 0.5 to 7.8 m²/g, no band gaps). Any train–test division must be by paper. The `pH` column is empty for sol-gel and combustion, so a pH-conditioned model can currently be fitted only on hydrothermal, co-precipitation and `other` rows.

Open-access solid-state TiO2 papers that report both a band gap and a BET value per sample are scarce in the searches run; the located candidates are listed in section 5 and the two with the most rows (Ganesh et al. 2012; Azeez et al. 2018) need manual retrieval.

- Added column `temperature_type` (values: calcination, hydrothermal, combustion) in response to the model-side request. All other columns and all 137 rows are identical to v3; the validator now rejects any other value.

Two assignments rest on indirect evidence and need a source check: paper 8 hydrothermal rows (200 °C, pressure about 13.8 bar, no calcination mentioned) and paper 14 (heat treatment 500 °C for 1 h, Section 2.2, taken as the combustion step).
- Counts by method and type: combustion 14 calcination + 11 combustion; hydrothermal 11 calcination + 14 hydrothermal; sol-gel, co-precipitation, solid-state and other are all `calcination`.
