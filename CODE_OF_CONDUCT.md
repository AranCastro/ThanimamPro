# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).

Participants are expected to be respectful, to accept technical criticism of their work, and to
keep discussion on the scientific and technical merits. Harassment, personal attacks and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Concerns may be reported to the maintainer through the contact listed on the maintainer's GitHub
profile (<https://github.com/AranCastro>). Reports are handled in confidence.
